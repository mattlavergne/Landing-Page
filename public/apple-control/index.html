How publishing works

01Design

Create a skin, select challenge mechanics, or schedule a tour. Work stays local until you save.

02Save draft

Keep your work safely in D1. A draft never changes what players see.

03Publish

Publish a versioned content snapshot. Players receive it at the next app launch, subject to connectivity and cache.

04Verify

Open the private playtest, inspect anonymous activity, and restore a prior version if necessary.

🍎 The Apple: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

The short version: The Apple has no accounts. It doesn't ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. If you say yes, the app counts a few anonymous things, like which levels players get stuck on; you can change your mind any time. Your progress stays on your device unless you turn on Sync. The free version of the app shows ads from Google that aren't personalized; buying the full game removes them.

Who we are

The Apple is made by Matt Lavergne, an individual developer in the United States (“we” in this policy). Email contact@mattlavergne.com. Write to us about anything in this policy, including requests to see or delete data.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, scores, upgrades, apple skins, settings, daily streaks and challenges) is saved on your device so you can pick up where you left off. In the app it is kept in the app's own storage, which your phone may include in its normal device backups. Deleting the app deletes it.

Sync (optional, off by default)

If you turn on Sync, the game makes up a random 12-character code and stores a copy of your game progress on our server at mattlavergne.com under that code, so another device that enters the same code can share it. That copy contains only game progress (levels, stars, scores, upgrades, skins, daily results), plus random labels the game makes up to keep each device's star count separate. Your settings stay on each device. It contains no name, email, phone number, advertising ID or hardware ID.

To protect your progress from mistakes, the server also keeps a few recent earlier versions of that copy (the last 10, plus one per day for 14 days). A cloud copy that hasn't been synced for 6 months is deleted automatically, along with those versions. Your devices keep their own progress either way.

  • Anyone who knows your sync code can load and change that save, so share it only with your own devices.
  • Like any internet service, our server (run on Cloudflare) sees your device's IP address in order to answer a sync request. We don't save it with your progress or use it to identify you; Cloudflare may keep it for a short time in its security logs to block attacks.
  • Delete cloud copy (on the Sync screen) deletes the cloud copy and its earlier versions right away. The server keeps only a note that the code was deleted (for up to 6 months), so other devices using it stop syncing; every device keeps its own progress.
  • “Stop syncing on this device” only stops that device from sending or receiving updates.

Ads (free version of the app)

The free version of the app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on menu screens, sometimes a full-screen ad between games (when you move on from a game over or a finished level), and ads you can choose to watch for bonus stars or a second chance. Never while you're playing a level.

  • How often full-screen ads come depends on how you play: new players and players having a hard time see none, and regular players see fewer. The game works this out on your device from your own play; it isn't sent to us or to Google, and it doesn't change which ads you see.
  • Ads are not personalized. They aren't based on your interests or what you do in other apps, and they're limited to content suitable for general audiences. Because children play too, every ad request tells Google to treat it as child-directed under COPPA: no advertising ID is sent and no interest-based or remarketing ads are shown. The app never asks to track you.
  • To show ads, count them and prevent fraud, Google's ad software collects some information from your device, such as your IP address, device type and settings, and an app-specific identifier, and records of the ads shown and tapped, plus crash and performance information about its ad software. Google uses it to deliver contextual ads, limit how often you see the same ad, count ads and fight fraud, under its own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • In the UK, the EEA and Switzerland, every ad request is also tagged as coming from someone under the age of consent, so Google shows only non-personalized ads there, sends nothing to other ad companies, and doesn't ask for ad consent.
  • Saw an ad that seems wrong for the game or for kids? Settings → Report an ad, and we'll block it.
  • Buying the full game turns ads off completely.

Anonymous play stats (in the app, only if you say yes)

After your first cleared level the app asks whether it may send anonymous play stats; nothing is sent unless you tap Sure. To see which levels are too hard, what brings players back and whether the Unlock screen makes sense, the app then counts a few moments of play: the first time it's opened, each day it's opened (with roughly how many days so far, like “4–7”), Adventure levels reached, cleared or lost (the level number), the first level ever cleared and the first time you're eaten, Daily Runs started and finished (which try and how far you got), the Share button, opening the Unlock screen (from where), purchases or restores (whether one happened, never who or how you paid), which lessons of the first-levels tutorial you've done, how soon you try again after losing, and when the app asked for a rating.

  • Each count is sent with only the date (not the time), the platform (iPhone or Android) and the app version. No device ID, advertising ID, sync code, name or location.
  • Our server only adds each one to a daily total, like “level 12 was cleared 40 times on October 20.” Nothing is stored per player or per device, so the totals can't be traced back to you.
  • Like any internet service, our server (run on Cloudflare) sees your device's IP address when the app sends counts. We don't save it; Cloudflare may keep it briefly in its security logs.
  • Turn it off any time: Settings → Share play stats. Counts not sent yet are deleted from your device, and nothing more is sent.

Daily Run ranking (optional, off until you choose it)

After a Daily Run you can tap Compare with everyone to see your rank for that day's Daily. The app then sends the Daily Run's number, your best score that day and the level you reached, and gets back your rank. Our server keeps only how many players got each score in each Daily, for 60 days. There are no names and no public list: only you see your rank, and the Share button can include it if you choose to share. Turn it off any time: Settings → Compare with everyone.

Reminders (optional, in the app)

After a Daily Run, the app may ask whether you'd like a reminder when the next one is ready. If you say yes (and allow notifications), it schedules a notification on your device around the time you usually play (worked out on your phone from when you start playing, and never sent anywhere), plus one in the evening only if your streak would end that night: at most two a day, and they pause after a few days without playing. The app creates these on your phone; nothing is sent to us or anyone else. Turn them off any time with Settings → Daily Run reminder, or in your phone's Settings.

Purchases

Buying the full game is handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only asks the store whether the full game was bought on your store account, and remembers the answer on your device. If anonymous play stats are on, the app counts that a purchase or restore happened, as described above; nothing else about purchases is sent to our server.

Sharing

The Share button only opens your device's own share sheet (or copies text) with a short result summary. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to send it. If anonymous play stats are on, the app counts that Share was tapped (not what you shared, or with whom).

Children (COPPA notice for parents)

The Apple is made to be fun for kids and adults, so we treat every player as possibly under 13. The game never asks for a name, email, photo, voice or location, and has no chat or links to social networks; a purchase always goes through the App Store's or Google Play's own confirmation.

The only information that could count as a child's personal information under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) is a persistent identifier: the IP address our server and Google see, the app-specific identifier Google's ad software uses, and the sync code if Sync is turned on. These are collected only for the internal operations of the game: delivering game data (network communications), syncing progress you asked us to sync, showing contextual (not personalized) ads and limiting how often they repeat, counting ads, and preventing fraud and keeping the service secure. We tag every ad request as child-directed, we don't use these identifiers to build a profile, show behavioral ads or contact anyone, and we don't sell them. Because we collect nothing else, the game relies on COPPA's exception for identifiers used only to support its internal operations, so it doesn't ask for parental consent.

Parents can ask us (contacts above) what we hold, delete the cloud copy (Settings → Sync → Delete cloud copy, or by email with the sync code), and turn off play stats and the Daily ranking in Settings.

Who receives data

  • Google LLC (AdMob), for the ads described above, free version only.
  • Cloudflare, Inc., which runs our server and stores the sync copies, play-stat totals and Daily ranking counts for us.
  • Apple and Google Play, which handle purchases and send nothing to us but whether the full game was bought.

That's everyone. We don't sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Each of these providers is bound by its terms with us to protect the data with the same or greater care than this policy describes. Our server and these providers may process data in the United States and other countries.

How long data is kept

Sync copies: until you delete them, or 6 months after the last sync. Daily ranking counts: 60 Dailies. Play stats: only daily totals, which identify no one, kept as long as they're useful. Report emails: as long as needed to answer and fix the problem, then deleted. Google keeps ad data under its own retention policy (linked above).

Your choices and rights

Everyone can turn off play stats, the Daily ranking and reminders in Settings, delete the cloud copy, and delete everything on the device by deleting the app. If you live in the UK, the EEA or Switzerland, you can also ask us for access to, correction or deletion of your data, object to its use, and complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO). Our legal bases: our legitimate interest in funding the free version with non-personalized, child-safe ads; your consent for play stats and the Daily ranking; providing the service you asked for, for Sync; and our legitimate interest in answering your emails. US residents can make the same requests by email.

Changes and contact
